Which Open-Model Harness Kit

Swapping the harness can move scores as much as swapping the model. If you run GLM, DeepSeek, Flint or another open model, the wrong wrapper burns money and the right one cuts cost per solved task.

The public Harness Leaderboard is free and useful. This kit is for people who want the leaderboard turned into installable recipes plus a short A/B on their repo — not another dashboard.

  • Decision tree: which harness to try first for your model and budget, based on published leaderboard pairs.
  • 3 install recipes: step-by-step setups for the top model↔harness pairs (not a fork of the board).
  • 5-task A/B: a small harness on your own repo so you compare two wrappers on the same work.
  • Cost sheet: cost per solved task so “cheaper model” does not hide an expensive harness.

We are measuring paid interest before building it. The free board may be enough for you — join only if you want the packaged recipes and local test.
